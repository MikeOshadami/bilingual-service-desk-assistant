# Service desk escalation procedure (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

This document is invented for the demo. It is not a real government procedure. It exists so the project can show
that staff-only knowledge is retrievable by authenticated staff and never by members of the public.

## When to escalate

- Escalate to a team lead when a resident reports that their licence was suspended in error.
- Escalate to the privacy officer within one business day when a resident reports that someone else received their documents.
- Escalate complaints that mention discrimination, safety or legal action to the client relations unit the same day.

## Escalation code words

- Use the internal tag ESC-BLUE for privacy incidents.
- Use the internal tag ESC-AMBER for urgent licence or registration errors that block someone from driving to work.
- Use the internal tag ESC-GREEN for routine complaints.

## Service standards

- Acknowledge every escalated case within 4 business hours.
- Record the escalation tag, the reason and the next action in the case notes.
- Never promise a resident a specific outcome before the team lead reviews the case.
